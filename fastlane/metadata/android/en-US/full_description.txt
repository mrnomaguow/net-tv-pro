NetTV Pro is a live TV player and cloud DVR studio for your phone. Watch free live streams, browse channels, check the programme guide, and schedule and keep recordings — all in one app.

Features:
- Live TV with HLS playback
- Preset free channels, plus search the public IPTV-org directory to find and add more stations
- Programme guide (EPG) for the current hour
- Scheduled recording and a DVR library stored on your device
- Import your own channels from an M3U playlist
- Prev / Next channel buttons, in the player controls and in a bar under the video
- Real fullscreen video, with a fallback in the page itself
- Respects the status bar and navigation bar — no content hidden under the clock or the menu bar

This app is only a player. The preset channels are other broadcasters' free public streams, and the Find Stations search lists streams from the public IPTV-org directory.
